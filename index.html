How this was made

Data

Monthly MODIS Terra NDVI (~1 km) and daytime land surface temperature (~5 km), 2001–2022, from NASA GIBS. GIBS serves colored map images, so values were recovered by reversing each layer's published color map. The region is 66–48°W, 14–5°S: Rondônia, northern Mato Grosso and southern Pará.

Clearing

A 1 km pixel is forest if its dry-season NDVI in 2001–03 was ≥ 0.75. It counts as cleared in year Y when its dry-season NDVI falls ≥ 0.20 below that level and stays down for 3 years. Clearing before 2004 is part of the baseline. Detections in 2021–22 are provisional because fewer later years exist to confirm them.

Comparison

Cleared = 5 km cells that lost ≥ 30% of their area as forest by 2022. Standing forest = cells that were ≥ 80% forest and lost < 5%. Both groups feel the same regional weather (droughts, El Niño), so the gap between them isolates the effect of clearing.

Caveats

A drop in NDVI can also mean fire or drought damage rather than clearing. The 2010 spike follows a severe drought and fire year. Daytime temperature is measured around 10:30 am, and the record stops in 2022 because the Terra satellite's orbit drifted after that. The pattern is consistent with clearing causing warming, but this is observational data.
